STAR CATCHER

Catch the falling stars. Dodge the bombs. Beat the clock.

How to play

  • 🕹️Move your catcher with ← → or A D, or move the mouse / drag your finger.
  • ⭐Star +10 points  ·  💎Gem +30 points
  • ❤️Heart restores a life (max 5)  ·  ⏱️Clock adds 6 seconds
  • 💣Bombs cost a life. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over!
  • 🔥Combo: every 5 catches in a row raises your multiplier (up to ×5). Missing a star resets it.
  • 🎯Fill the progress bar before time runs out to reach the next level. Leftover seconds become bonus points.
  • ⏸️Press P or Esc (or the pause button) to pause.
